## What we do

Wealth Marketing AI builds and runs client acquisition systems for three kinds of businesses: US coaches and consultants, real estate agencies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and US personal injury law firms. Paid ads, qualifying funnels, AI-assisted follow-up within minutes, and a CRM that tracks every lead to the sale. Coaches pay only on results. Agencies and law firms pay a flat monthly fee, never a percentage of fees.

## The three systems

Wealth Marketing AI is a client acquisition company, not a marketing agency. We build and run one complete system per client, from the ad to the booked conversation, and only work in three markets we know well.

### US coaches and consultants
The Speed to Win System books qualified sales calls onto the coach's calendar: paid ads, a short qualifier, AI-assisted follow-up within minutes, appointment setting and reminders. There is no retainer. We take 30% of every sale the system brings, invoiced monthly, and the client funds the ads.
