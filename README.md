## What we do

Wealth Marketing AI is a demand generation agency for categories that are hard to advertise. It plans and buys the paid media, builds the landing pages and qualifying funnels, follows up within minutes and tracks every lead to the sale, for four kinds of business: US mobility and durable medical equipment dealers, US personal injury (motor vehicle accident) law firms, US coaches and consultants, and real estate agencies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

There are two payment models and no others. A flat monthly fee: USD 3,500 a month for a mobility or DME dealer with one dealer per metro, USD 5,000 a month for a personal injury firm with one firm per metro and never a share of fees, and USD 5,000 a month for a Dubai or Abu Dhabi real estate agency with one agency per area, delivered with partner agency Progressive Solutions. Or a share of results with no retainer: 30% of every sale the system brings for coaches and consultants. The client funds the advertising spend in every case.

## Original research

We publish our own studies rather than repeat vendor claims. Every figure carries the crawl date, the method is published in full, only public pages are read, nothing is called or submitted, and no business is named for failing a signal.

- [The 9pm Test](https://wealthmarketing.ai/9pm-test): the method. A dated, reproducible check of what a person looking for help at 9pm actually finds on a business's public pages, scored across seven signals. Created by Juan Camilo at Wealth Marketing AI in 2026 and licensed for anyone to run and cite.
- [The 9pm Test, Los Angeles](https://wealthmarketing.ai/research/9pm-test-los-angeles): 160 personal injury firm websites read on 15 September 2026, 139 readable. 16 have a page actually written in Spanish.
- [The 9pm Test, Houston](https://wealthmarketing.ai/research/9pm-test-houston): 148 firm websites read the same day, 142 readable. 23 have a page actually written in Spanish. No firm in either metro scored above 5 of 7.
- [The Stairlift Enquiry Study](https://wealthmarketing.ai/research/stairlift-enquiry-study): ten signals across 596 US mobility and home medical equipment dealer websites, 567 readable. 157 publish a price a buyer can read.
- [The Coach Funnel Review](https://wealthmarketing.ai/research/coach-funnel-study): eight signals across 483 US coach and consultant websites, 444 readable. 97 have no booking link at all.
- [Law firm intake statistics, 2026](https://wealthmarketing.ai/blog/law-firm-intake-statistics): 30 sourced numbers, each with its publisher, its year and a link.

The aggregate data behind the two personal injury runs is deposited under CC BY 4.0 at [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22815426](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22815426) and mirrored at [doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33898819](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33898819).

## The four systems

Every client owns the accounts, pages and data we build.

### US mobility and home medical equipment dealers
Meta and Google demand for cash-pay products (stairlifts, scooters, lift chairs, ramps), a four-question qualifying page before a rep calls, booking and tracking to the sale. No Medicare claims, no medical advice, no health claims in creative. One dealer per metro, flat USD 3,500 a month, never per lead.

Offer page: [https://wealthmarketing.ai/mobility](https://wealthmarketing.ai/mobility)

### US personal injury law firms
A Spanish-first case acquisition system: Google Search in native Spanish and English, qualifying pages, speed-to-lead intake automation and a CRM that tracks every case to the signed retainer. One firm per metro, flat USD 5,000 a month, never a share of fees (structured for ABA Model Rule 5.4).

Offer page: [https://wealthmarketing.ai/lawyers](https://wealthmarketing.ai/lawyers)
Qualifier: [https://wealthmarketing.ai/lawyers/quiz](https://wealthmarketing.ai/lawyers/quiz)

### US coaches and consultants
Paid ads, a short qualifier, AI-assisted follow-up within minutes, appointment setting and reminders, so qualified sales calls land on the coach's calendar. No retainer. 30% of every sale the system brings, invoiced monthly, and the client funds the ads.

Offer page: [https://wealthmarketing.ai/coaches](https://wealthmarketing.ai/coaches)
Qualifier: [https://wealthmarketing.ai/coaches/quiz](https://wealthmarketing.ai/coaches/quiz)

### Real estate agencies in Dubai and Abu Dhabi
Exclusive buyer appointments, one agency per area, delivered with technology partner Progressive Solutions, whose AI captures, qualifies and follows up every enquiry. Flat USD 5,000 a month (AED 18,350); the agency funds the media.
